// ABOUT THE COMPANY

A corporate-grade ISP, built by network engineers.

Dhaka Fiber Link Ltd. is a comprehensive internet service and IT solution provider operating in Bangladesh's IT market, connected through an underground NTTN optical fiber backbone with full redundancy across the network core.

A dedicated NOC team

A round-the-clock Network Operation Centre staffed by trained engineers keeps the network secure, monitored, and continuously updated.

Carrier-grade backbone

Core network deployed on dark fiber with multiple redundant high-capacity links and carrier-grade routing and switching hardware.

Built for continuous improvement

A standing commitment to raise service quality to international standards, backed by experienced technical consultants.

Why corporate clients choose us

We supply affordable, high-speed, easy-to-set-up broadband across Uttara and Banani, with corporate connectivity available across the whole of Dhaka Division.

Our objective is simple: make reliable, hassle-free, high-quality internet access available to every corporate and home customer we serve, without compromising on uptime or support response time.

We're always open to extending our underground fiber backbone to new sites — reach out and our team will scope a connection plan for your location.

// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which areas do you cover?

Home internet is available in Uttara, Gulshan-1/2, Banani, Nikunja, and Baridhara J-Block. Corporate connectivity is available across the whole of Dhaka Division.

How fast can I get a new connection installed?

Most home installations are completed within a few business days of confirming coverage at your address. Corporate sites are scoped individually based on backbone proximity.

Is the internet really unlimited?

Yes — every home package includes 24/7/365 unlimited use with no FUP cap, delivered over a dedicated fiber optic connection.

What makes your network reliable?

We're connected through 3 redundant IIG links and an underground NTTN fiber backbone, making it practically impossible for the main link to go down, monitored by our in-house NOC team.

Do you offer business-grade SLAs?

Yes — our Business package includes dedicated bandwidth, static IP, a corporate SLA, and priority support, with pricing scoped to your requirements.
